How ACAP works

A team of persistent agents.

Independent preparation · ordered access

Work in parallel.
Respect physical dependencies.

Members prepare independently.The hyper-agent coordinates access to shared spaces.

Coupled manipulation

More agents alone
are not enough.

Both arms must coordinate their motions when handling the same object.The hyper-agent chooses between delegation and direct lead control.

Less coordination overhead.

Fixed coordinates coupled actions through separate member agents, repeatedly updating and synchronizing their plans. ACAP can bring those actions under direct lead control.

In towel folding and carton lifting, Fixed overlaps more reasoning but also spends longer waiting for coordination.
